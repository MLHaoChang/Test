Why did my portfolio perform like this?

In plain language

Over the last year your portfolio returned 14.2% against 11.8% for the MSCI World in euros, so you finished 2.4 points ahead. Most of that came from picking well inside technology (NVIDIA and ASML beat their sector by a wide margin: about 1.9 points). Being overweight technology added about 0.9 points because the sector beat the market. The weaker US dollar took back 0.8 points on your US holdings. Novo Nordisk was the single worst decision at minus 1.1 points. Fees and spreads cost 0.2 points. Put simply: you won by holding two chip companies through a strong year, and you paid for it with more concentration than your own limits allow.

!One year is a short window. Two positions explain most of the result. Over three years the same portfolio trailed the benchmark by 2.5 points; see the 3Y view before drawing conclusions.
