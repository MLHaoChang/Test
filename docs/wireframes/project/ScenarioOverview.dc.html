What the assistant has learned from your scenarios

Weekly, from the differences between every scenario and the base case, and where each difference came from (sector, stock, currency, rule timing, costs).

Across five scenarios, what you own by sector mattered more than which stock you picked inside it: S1's lead is almost all semiconductor exposure (sector effect +1.9 pts, stock effect +0.1) and S2's swap inside software changed nothing. Both trend filters lose in a rising market, by 1.2 to 2.6 points a year, and pay for themselves only in falls deeper than about 20%; there has been one such fall in the five years tested. Rebalancing cadence is invisible over weeks.

How the comparison stays fair

Same days, same prices, same cost and tax model. Your deposits and withdrawals are removed from the base case. Forward scenarios only ever see past data. History scenarios carry a hindsight warning when they name specific stocks.
