Why S1 won, and what it cost

written by the assistant from the run data, sources linked

S1 ends 7,100 EUR ahead of your real portfolio, and almost all of it comes from one decision: adding two chip makers before the 2023 to 2025 semiconductor cycle. ASML and TSMC returned 3.1 times and 2.6 times over the period against 1.6 times for the world index [1]. The price was a deeper fall in 2022 (minus 33% against minus 27%) and three more months to recover. S2 barely differs from the real portfolio: Microsoft and SAP moved together most of the time, so the swap mainly generated a taxable gain of about 1,500 EUR at the swap date [2]. S3 lost the least in 2022 because the trend filter sold in February, but it missed the first four months of the 2023 recovery, which is where the trend filter usually pays for itself over long periods and did not here [3].

!Hindsight warning. You built S1 in 2026 knowing how chips did. A five-year test of a stock you already know went up is not evidence the rule that picked it works. Run the same rule on a period you did not look at, or let a scenario agent run it forward from today.
!Outside your limits. S1 puts technology at 49% against your 35% sector limit, and TSMC at 12% against your 10% single-position limit. The comparison still runs; a proposal from it would be flagged.
