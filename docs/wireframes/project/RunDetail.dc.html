This strategy held SPY when its price was above its 10-month average and Treasury bills otherwise. Over 19 years it returned 8.6% a year against 11.1% for simply holding SPY, so it gave up about 2.5 points a year. In exchange, its worst fall was 21.8% instead of 51%. Risk-adjusted, the two are about equal (Sharpe 0.69 against 0.67). It traded 34 times, roughly 1.6 switches a year, and costs took 3% of gross profit. This is a defensive result, not an outperforming one.
