Why did the trend-filter scenario (S3) do worse than just holding over the last five years? I thought trend following protects you.
It did protect you, in 2022: S3 fell 14% from its peak against 27% for your real portfolio, because the filter sold in February 2022 when the world index dropped below its 10-month average [1]. It lost the race afterwards for two reasons.

1. It re-entered late. The filter bought back in April 2023, after the index had already risen 12% from the October low. Missing those months cost about 6 points [2].
2. Two false alarms. In late 2023 and mid 2025 it sold and re-bought within two to three months, each time slightly lower than it sold, costing roughly 1.5 points plus about 600 EUR in realised taxes [3].

This is the normal shape of a trend filter: it gives up some return in most years to avoid the rare very large fall. Over 5 years with one moderate bear market, that trade-off did not pay. Over 1970 to 2025 it did, on average, but with long stretches of underperformance [4]. Whether you want it depends on how much a 30% fall would bother you, which your profile currently puts at "uncomfortable above 25%".
